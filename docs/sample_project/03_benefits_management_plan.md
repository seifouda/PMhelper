## 1. Purpose

This Benefits Management Plan defines how EduTech Dynamics will identify, measure,
track, and confirm the benefits associated with the PMHelper_Edu Development Project.
It establishes ownership, measurement criteria, and a monitoring schedule that extends
beyond the project completion date, covering the full five-year benefit horizon defined
in the Business Case (PMHE-2025-BC-001).

The plan ensures that project success is not evaluated solely on delivery of the product,
but on realisation of the intended business and strategic value.

---

### B2 — Faculty Time Savings (Avoided Cost)

## 7. Benefits Assumptions and Constraints

| #   | Assumption / Constraint                                                         | Impact if Wrong                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BA1 | Product achieves ≥ 3 institutional adoptions in Year 1                          | B1, B2 delayed; financial model deteriorates                                 |
| BA2 | EGP/USD exchange rate remains approximately stable                              | USD-denominated subscriptions (Render, GitHub) remain within budget          |
| BA3 | PMI certification interest in Egypt continues growing at current rate           | CAPM/PMP candidate market for B4/B5 may shrink                               |
| BA4 | Open-source MIT license does not attract a competing fork that diverts adoption | B1, B3 revenue cannibalized                                                  |
| BA5 | EduTech Dynamics invests ≥ EGP 60,000/year in product maintenance post-release  | Without maintenance, product degrades; adoption declines; all benefits erode |
