## 1. Purpose

This Stakeholder Register identifies all individuals, groups, and organisations that
may affect, be affected by, or perceive themselves to be affected by the PMHelper_Edu
Development Project. It documents each stakeholder's interests, influence, current
engagement level, desired engagement level, and the strategy for maintaining productive
engagement throughout the project lifecycle.

The register is a living document. New stakeholders are added as they are identified.
Engagement levels are reassessed monthly and at each milestone.
